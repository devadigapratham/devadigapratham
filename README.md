I'm a first-year PhD student at [Dartmouth](https://web.cs.dartmouth.edu/) in the [JASPR Lab](https://www.jasprlab.com/), advised by [Shawn Shan](https://www.shawnshan.com). I work on the security, privacy, and trustworthiness of generative AI: what models reveal about their training data, whether we can verify what a model learned or unlearned, and how deployed systems fail under attack.
